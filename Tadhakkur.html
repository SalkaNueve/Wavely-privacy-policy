Privacy Policy

Effective August 1, 2026
In short

Tadhakkur collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you set or save stays on your device, and the app works entirely offline.

About Tadhakkur

Tadhakkur is a daily Qur'anic reminder app. It shows one verse each day in Arabic with an English translation, lets you browse verses by theme, save the ones that stay with you, and share them as an image.

Tadhakkur is developed and published by SalkaNueve. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use the app.

Data we collect

We do not collect any personal data. Everything you enter into Tadhakkur — your name, the themes you choose, your reminder time and the verses you save — is stored exclusively on your device.

We have no servers, no databases and no analytics infrastructure. We have no way to read your data, even if we wanted to.

Information stored on your device

The following is stored locally and is never transmitted anywhere:

Your name. Optional, and used only to greet you on the main screen.

Your preferences. The themes you would like to be reminded of, your reminder time, your chosen visual theme, and the focus setting if you use it.

Saved verses. Stored using Apple's SwiftData framework. Only the reference is kept — the verse text always comes from inside the app.

The app works offline

Tadhakkur makes no network requests while you use it. Every verse is already inside the app when you download it, and the daily reminder is worked out on your device from the date alone.

This means the app never sends a request that could reveal what you are reading, when you open it, or where you are.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Tadhakkur schedules local notifications on your device. These are handled entirely by iOS and are never routed through any external server. We do not use push notifications and do not receive notification tokens.

Widget

The home screen widget shows the shared daily verse. It works out which verse to show on your device, using nothing but the date. It does not read your preferences or your saved verses, and it makes no network requests.

Subscriptions and payments

Tadhakkur offers an optional premium subscription processed entirely through Apple's App Store and StoreKit framework. We do not process, store or have access to any payment information, card numbers or Apple Account details.

Apple tells us only whether a subscription is currently active. Please refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for how Apple handles your purchase data.

Third-party services

Tadhakkur contains no third-party analytics, advertising, tracking or data collection software. No Firebase, no Google Analytics, no Facebook SDK, no attribution or crash-reporting services of any kind.

The only Apple frameworks involved are StoreKit for subscriptions and WidgetKit for the home screen widget, both of which run on your device.

Sharing

When you share a verse, the image and text are handed to Apple's standard share sheet and go wherever you send them. We are not involved in that and receive no record of it.

We do not share, sell, rent or trade your personal information with anyone.

Data deletion

Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. There is nothing held elsewhere for us to delete, and nothing to request from us.

Children's privacy

Tadhakkur does not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13. The app collects no information at all, and asks only for a first name, which is optional and used solely to personalise a greeting.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
